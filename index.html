NEW TO IT?

IT means using computers and software to solve real problems: the apps on your phone, the websites you visit, the systems banks and hospitals rely on. You do not need to know coding yet. Start by exploring the six main areas below.

Software Development

Building the programs and apps people use every day.

Web Development

Creating websites and web apps that run in the browser.

Data

Collecting and studying information to help make decisions.

Design

Making products look good and feel easy to use.

Testing

Checking software carefully so bugs are found before users see them.

Cloud & Technology

Running and protecting the systems that keep apps online.

READY FOR YOUR FIRST INTERVIEW?

Interviews are easier when you know what to expect. Prepare these seven areas.

Technical Questions

Explain concepts in your own words instead of memorizing answers.

Programming Basics

Loops, conditions, functions and simple problem solving.

SQL

Write basic queries: select, filter, join and group.

Projects

Be ready to explain what you built, why and what went wrong.

Resume

Keep it to one page with your skills, projects and education.

HR Questions

Practice answers to "Tell me about yourself" and "Why this role?"

Communication

Speak clearly, ask questions and stay calm when you are unsure.
